Problem Statement

Carrying out manual billing in a restaurant can be time consuming and might result in mistakes when doing the calculations. This project simplifies the billing process by automatically working out the bill.

Scope of the Project

The system deals with simple restaurant billing,including the selection of items, entering the quantity, the application of discounts, the addition of GST and the calculation of the final bill.

Target Users

- Restaurant staff
- Cashiers
- Small restaurant owners
- Students learning Python

High-Level Features

- Display food items and prices
- Select items and enter quantities
- Calculate the total amount
- Apply discount and GST
- Generate the final bill
- Display customer and billing details
